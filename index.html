About

With over 20 years in solution architecture and technology leadership across fintech, telehealth, e-commerce, ERP, and supply chain, I connect the boardroom and the engineering room — framing the business case and building the prototype.

I'm moving toward Forward-Deployed AI Architect roles, working shoulder to shoulder with customers from the first design-thinking workshop to live deployment, with a focus on agentic AI and responsible enterprise GenAI.

  • End-to-end ownership: find the right problems with CXOs, design the solution, prove it fast with working PoCs (GenAI, RAG, agents, APIs, cloud-native), and take it to production with security, governance and reliability from day one.
  • Pre-sales to delivery bridge: I understand both what is promised and what it takes to deliver.
  • Capability building: I mentor developers, testers, designers and PMs so solutions live on after my involvement.
  • Design Thinking & IBM Garage practices applied for requirements, feasibility, prototyping and architecture.
